## Unearned rungs

### RIL-3 — provenance-separated generalization: NOT OPENED

RIL-002 is not sufficient because SEM8 was originally selected with knowledge of FS007 structure. A real RIL-3 study must freeze a representation-selection process using only allowed selection information, then expose genuinely held-out correction information after the representation becomes immutable.

Candidate firewall:

\[
\mathcal I_{select}(R) \cap \mathcal I_{test}=\varnothing
\]

or a prospectively defined operational equivalent that establishes causal/provenance separation.

A RIL-3 positive would require leverage on held-out transformations plus per-member preservation. It must not use RIL-002 outcomes to tune the representation that is then tested on the same information.

### RIL-4 — resource-boundary amplification: UNOPENED

Candidate object:

\[
\Omega_{eff}(R;B)=\{\omega:\tau_R(\omega)\text{ valid and }c_R(\omega)\le B\}.
\]

The conjecture is that a representation can have especially large effective-capability consequences near a fixed resource boundary:

```text
c_R0(omega) > B >= c_R1(omega)
```

RIL-001/002 show cost reduction, not this boundary-crossing effect. A future assay must manipulate or freeze `B` explicitly.

### RIL-5 — broad effective generality on constrained substrate: UNOPENED

Maximal conjecture:

> A representation may place a surprisingly broad class of valid typed transformations below the practical resource boundary of a fixed constrained substrate.

This remains far beyond current evidence.

## Candidate affordance notation

The provisional object remains:

\[
\mathcal A(R)=(\Omega_R,\tau_R,c_R,\mathcal C_R),
\]

where `Omega_R` is a transformation repertoire, `tau_R` semantic/type signatures, `c_R` realization cost, and `C_R` legal constraints.

This notation is useful for asking questions; RIL-001 and RIL-002 do not establish it as a universal ontology.

## Representation payback horizon

Construction/learning cost remains part of any later reusable-representation claim:

\[
C_{total}(R,n)=C_{learn}(R)+\sum_{i=1}^{n}c_R(F_i).
\]

Candidate payback horizon:

\[
n^*=\min\{n:C_{total}(R_1,n)<C_{total}(R_0,n)\}.
\]

Neither RIL-001 nor RIL-002 tested learned representation cost or payback horizon because SEM8 already existed in the frozen parent representation libraries.

```text

```

It does not yet establish one unified theory of those questions.

## Escalation rule

```text
single-function leverage != family transfer
family transfer != provenance-separated generalization
provenance-separated generalization != resource-boundary amplification
resource-boundary amplification != broad effective generality
```

Every rung requires a new prospective contract, preservation gate, resource accounting, null, and claim ceiling.
