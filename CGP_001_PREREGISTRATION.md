Every NSS-using arm independently recomputes translation and the four unrotated NSS decisions in its own fresh child. It materializes that vector before any downstream FS M1 branch. For the geometry vector, rotation gives `[I,I,I,S]`. A valid shuffled misallocation is `SKIP` on condition 0 or 3 or `INVOKE` on condition 2. A vector with all four decisions identical is non-discriminating and cannot satisfy localization. Vector or multiset inequality invalidates the control; it is not a successful misallocation.

For a fresh condition, the CGP `ConditionResult` wrapper records `SKIP` exactly as:

